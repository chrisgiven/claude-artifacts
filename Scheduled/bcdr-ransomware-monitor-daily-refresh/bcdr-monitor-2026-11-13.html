A global Microsoft 365 outage began on November 12. It peaked at about 140,000 Downdetector reports from 74 countries, with Exchange Online mailbox access failing. Recovery was finished in Asia-Pacific first and was still being extended to other regions on November 13 (EX1550543 / MO1550821). CNBC

M365 outage spills into Teams, SharePoint, Copilot and Defender XDRCloudThird-party

Reports of the outage broadened to Teams, SharePoint, Copilot and Defender XDR, and to UK services. Third-party M365 integrations, including Claude's, were also affected. Cybersecurity News

Cloudflare Durable Objects incidentCloud

Cloudflare had a separate, brief incident on November 13 involving errors in its Durable Objects service, fixed quickly. There is no confirmed link to the Microsoft outage. NewsBytes

AWS denies outage reportsReference

Amazon said AWS was working normally all day and blamed an unrelated disruption elsewhere for inaccurate news reports. It is a reminder to check provider health dashboards before invoking a DR plan. Macworld
